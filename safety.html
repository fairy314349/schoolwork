SECTION 00执行摘要Executive Summary

六条核心判断Six core judgements

  1. 安全目标数量级跃升：飞行汽车面向载人飞行，其航空级安全要求远高于地面交通。课件第五章以 10⁻⁹ 量级的灾难性失效概率作为对照，指出"技术同源但体系不等价"——新能源汽车三电与无人机飞控虽高度同源，仍必须按航空适航体系做系统级重构[21]。Order-of-magnitude shift: passenger flight demands aviation-grade safety, with catastrophic failure probability on the order of 10⁻⁹. Automotive and UAV technologies are highly homologous but not equivalent; a system-level reconstruction against airworthiness requirements is mandatory [21].
  2. 无人驾驶重构了风险分布：取消机载驾驶员消除了人为操纵失误这一传统主因（课件引用 NHTSA：道路事故 90% 以上与人为因素有关[21]），但把风险转移到预设航线、地面调度、通信链路与应急逻辑。安全边界从"单机"扩展为"机—地—网—管"体系。Pilotless operation redistributes risk: removing the on-board pilot eliminates human error (NHTSA: >90% of road crashes involve human factors [21]) but transfers risk to pre-loaded routes, ground dispatch, data links and contingency logic. The safety boundary becomes a system of systems.
  3. 五类安全缺一不可：被动安全、功能安全（ISO 26262 思路 / ARP4754A、ARP4761）、预期功能安全（ISO 21448）、行为安全（IEEE P2846 类假设）、信息安全（ISO/SAE 21434、TARA）构成完整拼图；课件第五章明确将"由内而外的安全关键复杂系统工程"作为统合框架[21]。Five safety classes are jointly necessary: passive, functional, SOTIF, behavioural and cybersecurity safety, unified under the "safety-critical complex systems engineering from the inside out" framing of Chapter 5 [21].
  4. 全备份不等于无共因失效：8 轴 16 旋翼、多组电池、多余度飞控提供了充分的冗余资源，但真正的适航难点在于共因分析（CCA）、故障传播机理与重构控制律的验证，以及全机系统安全性分析（FHA / PSSA / SSA / FTA）的证据链完整性[21]。Redundancy is not immunity: sixteen rotors, multiple battery groups and redundant flight computers provide resources, but the certification challenge lies in common-cause analysis, failure propagation, reconfiguration control laws and a complete FHA/PSSA/SSA/FTA evidence chain [21].
  5. SOTIF 是本构型最薄弱的一环：低空复杂气象（雷暴、强风、大雨）、GNSS 遮挡与多路径、突发障碍物、链路时延以及乘客误用，属于"功能正确但性能不足"的风险，无法靠冗余硬件消除，只能靠运行设计条件（ODC）约束、场景化验证与触发条件识别[21][14]。SOTIF is the weakest link: low-altitude weather, GNSS occlusion, obstacles, link latency and passenger misuse are performance-limitation risks that redundancy cannot remove; they require ODC constraints, scenario-based validation and triggering-condition identification [21][14].
  6. 结论分层：限定 ODC 内的设计安全性已通过局方审定；但公开资料不足以支持"高频次、多站点、复杂气象下运行安全已充分验证"的结论。安全论证是一个需要运行数据持续喂养的生命周期过程，而非一次取证即可封存的结果[21][22]。Layered conclusion: design safety within a limited ODC has been accepted by the regulator; public evidence does not support a claim of fully validated safety under high-frequency, multi-site, complex-weather operations. The safety case is a lifecycle process fed by operating data, not a one-off certification artefact [21][22].

1.1　从"人—车—环境"到"机—地—网—管"1.1 From human–vehicle–environment to aircraft–ground–network–regulator

课件以"人—车—环境"系统视角分析自动驾驶汽车的安全挑战，并指出传统车与自动化车辆在感知、决策、执行、接管上的差异；当自动化等级提高，车辆自身导致的事故占比会上升[21]。EH216-S 把这一逻辑推到极致：机上既没有驾驶员也没有接管主体，控制动作由飞控系统发出，运行约束由地面调度系统与法规共同施加。因此本页把分析对象从三层扩展为四域。

Chapter 5 analyses automated driving through the human–vehicle–environment lens and notes that automation shifts accident causation toward the vehicle itself [21]. EH216-S removes both the pilot and any takeover agent: control actions come from the flight-control system while constraints are imposed by ground dispatch and regulation. Four domains are therefore analysed.

SECTION 02运行设计条件与场景定义Operational Design Conditions and Scenario Definition

课件在方法解析中把"基于运行设计条件（ODC）的场景分析"列为第 1 步的核心，并把"ODD 退出、性能局限"列为必须识别的风险来源[21]。对预设航线无人驾驶构型而言，ODC 不只是"天气好不好"，而是一组可审计的边界条件：一旦任一条件被突破，系统必须进入预先定义的应急逻辑，而不是继续按原航线飞行。

Chapter 5 places ODC-based scenario analysis at the core of step 1 and identifies ODD exit and performance limitations as risk sources [21]. For a pilotless pre-programmed aircraft, the ODC is a set of auditable boundary conditions: breaching any one must trigger predefined contingency logic rather than continued route-following.

为什么"链路中断"必须按失效处理，而不是按"通信不便"处理Why link loss is a failure, not an inconvenience

在有人驾驶航空器中，通信中断时驾驶员仍可独立完成飞行；在 EH216-S 这类构型中，地面调度承担着航线管理、运行监控与应急决策的部分职能。因此链路完整性属于安全关键功能，其失效必须纳入 FHA 与 FTA，并用机载自主应急逻辑作为最后屏障。这是"取消驾驶员"带来的最典型的安全需求转移。

In piloted aviation, a pilot can continue independently after losing communications. Here the ground centre shares route management, monitoring and contingency decisions, making link integrity safety-critical and requiring on-board autonomous contingency logic as the last barrier.

3.1　STPA 视角：把"没有遵守"改成"控制动作不安全"3.1 An STPA view: unsafe control actions, not component failures

课件以 STPA 控制结构分析自动驾驶系统，强调控制动作（Control Action）与反馈（Feedback）在系统层级上的交互[21]。对 EH216-S，这一视角尤其重要：许多风险并非来自部件坏了，而是来自控制动作在不该发出时发出、该发出时未发出、或发出过早/过晚/持续过久——例如地面调度在气象已越界时仍下发起飞许可、飞控在链路中断后继续执行旧航线、推力重构指令在传感器漂移下被错误触发。

Chapter 5 uses an STPA control structure with control actions and feedback [21]. Many risks here are unsafe control actions rather than component failures: a dispatch release issued after weather limits are exceeded, continued execution of a stale route after link loss, or thrust reconfiguration triggered by drifting sensors.

SECTION 04功能安全与失效分析Functional Safety and Failure Analysis

课件把功能安全定义为"降低电子电气故障带来的安全风险"：通过安全分析、ASIL 分解与安全机制，使系统在足够短的时间内探测到危害事件并回到安全状态，把新增 E/E 功能带来的风险降到可接受范围[21]。在航空侧，对应的是 ARP4754A 的研制保证体系与 ARP4761 的 FHA / PSSA / SSA 安全性评估流程[18]。本节把两者并列使用：汽车侧提供"故障—安全机制"的思路，航空侧提供"自上而下、逐层分解"的证据结构。

Chapter 5 defines functional safety as reducing risk from E/E failures through safety analysis, ASIL decomposition and safety mechanisms that bring the system to a safe state in time [21]. On the aviation side, ARP4754A development assurance and ARP4761 FHA/PSSA/SSA provide a top-down evidence structure [18].

STEP 01FHA 功能危害性评估Functional Hazard Assessment

自上而下识别功能失效状态与严酷度，形成顶层安全目标。Top-down identification of failure conditions and severity.

失效—安全 还是 失效—运行？Fail-safe or fail-operational?

地面车辆在严重故障时通常允许"安全停车"（fail-safe / fail-shutdown）；但载人航空器在巡航阶段没有"靠边停车"这一选项，必须在故障后继续维持可控飞行直到着陆（fail-operational）。课件在典型 ADS 功能表中也区分了 L2+ 的 fail-shutdown 与 L3 的 fail-operational[21]。这一差异是 EH216-S 必须采用全备份动力、多组电池与推力重构逻辑的根本原因，也是其安全架构区别于车规设计的核心。

A road vehicle may safely stop after a severe failure; a passenger aircraft in flight has no "pull over" option and must remain controllable until landing (fail-operational). Chapter 5 makes the same distinction between L2+ fail-shutdown and L3 fail-operational [21]. This is the root reason for full propulsion redundancy and thrust reallocation.

SECTION 05预期功能安全 SOTIFSafety of the Intended Functionality (SOTIF)

课件第五章指出："处理了 E/E 故障，就安全了么？"——每一次接管，都可能是一次交通事故。加州 DMV 接管报告所列原因包括环境感知能力不足、决策逻辑问题、车载软硬件故障、数据传输迟滞、GPS 定位与传感器配合、环境复杂度超出能力等，其中相当一部分并非故障，而是功能在既定场景下表现不足[21]。ISO 21448 把这类风险定义为"由于性能局限及人为误用带来的风险"[14]。

Chapter 5 asks: does handling E/E failures make a system safe? Disengagement reports cite perception limits, decision logic, latency, GPS and sensor coordination, and environmental complexity — many are performance insufficiencies rather than failures [21]. ISO 21448 addresses risks from performance limitations and reasonably foreseeable misuse [14].

SECTION 06信息安全与电磁兼容Cybersecurity and Electromagnetic Compatibility

课件第五章把信息安全定义为"降低外部攻击带来的风险"，以 ISO/SAE 21434 为基础，在功能安全开发流程中加入威胁分析与风险评估（TARA）及验证过程[17][21]；并把"防止网络安全威胁、电磁攻击与侵入"列为飞行汽车设计阶段必须考虑的项目之一[21]。对无人驾驶构型，信息安全不是附加功能，而是功能安全的前置条件：一条被伪造的放行指令，等价于一个安全关键的错误控制动作。

Chapter 5 defines cybersecurity as reducing risk from external attacks through ISO/SAE 21434 and TARA integrated into the safety lifecycle [17][21], and lists protection against cyber threats, electromagnetic attacks and intrusions among design-stage considerations. For a pilotless aircraft, a forged dispatch command is equivalent to an unsafe control action.

电磁兼容为什么属于安全而非"体验"问题Why EMC is a safety issue, not a comfort issue

课件把"信息安全与电磁兼容"并列为飞行汽车潜在研究点之一[21]。城市低空环境中存在密集的通信基站、广播发射与高压设施；强电磁场可能同时影响导航接收、链路与传感器，形成典型的共因干扰。因此 CCA 不仅要覆盖硬件共因，也要覆盖"同一电磁环境同时作用于多个冗余通道"的情形。

Chapter 5 lists cybersecurity and EMC together as research topics [21]. Dense urban electromagnetic environments can affect navigation, links and sensors simultaneously — a common-cause disturbance that CCA must cover alongside hardware common causes.

SECTION 08行为安全与人为因素Behavioural Safety and Human Factors

课件把行为安全定义为"降低由于周围交通参与者交互带来的风险"，并以 IEEE P2846《安全相关自动化车辆行为假设》为例，列举安全距离、切入、路权、视野受限四类典型交互[27][21]。虽然 EH216-S 运行在隔离空域、不与其他交通参与者共享路权，但"人"并未从系统中消失，只是换了位置：地面调度操作员、维护放行人员、乘客、起降场管理人员、以及应急恢复人员，共同构成新的行为安全边界。

Chapter 5 defines behavioural safety as reducing risk from interactions with other traffic participants, citing IEEE P2846 assumptions on safe distance, cut-in, right of way and limited visibility [27][21]. In segregated airspace the human is not removed but relocated: ground operators, maintenance release staff, passengers, site staff and recovery crews form the new behavioural boundary.

本文结论 · 课程作业 02Concluding remark · Coursework 02

总结：把课件第五章的系统安全工程过程完整套用到 EH216-S，可以得到一个清晰的结论。第一，构型层面的安全资源是充分的：16 个独立动力单元、关键部件全备份、地面调度与预设航线，构成可支撑"故障后维持可控飞行"的物理基础。第二，真正的难点不在冗余数量，而在共因失效、SOTIF 触发条件、链路完整性、垂直坠撞适撞性与运行体系的证据链。第三，四证齐全证明的是限定 ODC 内的设计安全性已获局方接受，而非"任何场景下的运行安全已被证明"。第四，安全是一个生命周期过程：从 FHA 到 SSA、从场景库到运行数据、从 GSN 到持续适航，都需要随运行规模扩大而持续更新。这也正是课件把"全生命周期安全保障技术"列为飞行汽车支撑技术的原因[21]。

Conclusion: applying the full Chapter 5 process yields four findings. (1) Configuration-level safety resources are substantial: sixteen independent propulsion units, full redundancy and ground-supervised pre-programmed routes. (2) The hard problems are common-cause failures, SOTIF triggering conditions, link integrity, vertical crashworthiness and the operational evidence chain — not the number of redundant parts. (3) Four approvals demonstrate accepted design safety within a limited ODC, not proven safety in all scenarios. (4) Safety is a lifecycle process, which is why Chapter 5 lists whole-lifecycle safety assurance as an enabling technology [21].
